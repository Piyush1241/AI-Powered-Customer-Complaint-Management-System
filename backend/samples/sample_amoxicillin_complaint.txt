COMPLAINT DESCRIPTION & QUALITY DEFECT DETAILED OBSERVATIONS:
During routine dispensing at the pediatric outpatient clinic, pharmacy staff observed severe discoloration in multiple blister packages from Lot #AMX-2024-09A. The standard opaque white/blue capsules exhibited mottled brownish specks, localized swelling, and clumping within the aluminum foil blisters. Upon opening 3 sample bottles, a faint sour odor was noted.

No adverse clinical events have been reported to date, but affected bottles have been removed from clinical inventory immediately. Quarantine tags have been attached. Requesting immediate investigation, QA disposition, and credit/replacement stock.
